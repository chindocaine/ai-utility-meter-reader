1. Reference image

Upload a clean, well-lit photo of the meter. This defines the coordinate space for every digit ROI below.

Correct perspective (optional)

For a camera mounted off-axis to the meter display: drag out the display's 4 corners in order (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left) - drop a corner with a click, then drag it before releasing to fine-tune, and drag any already-placed corner again any time to adjust it. The whole image is then warped so the traced area becomes rectangular (nothing is cropped away). Nothing is saved until you click "Apply perspective correction", which bakes it into the reference image and clears any existing ROI boxes. Alignment against live photos already uses a full homography, so it keeps working automatically after this.

Straighten (optional)

Preview a rotation to correct camera skew before drawing ROIs - it's much easier to line up horizontal digit boxes on a de-skewed image. Nothing is saved until you click "Apply rotation", which bakes it into the reference image and clears any existing ROI boxes (their old coordinates would no longer line up).
